Privacy Policy

```

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Football Schedule Alerts 2026 does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Data Collection

The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, location data, contacts, photos, health data, financial information, or identifiers for tracking.

Local Data

The app stores user-entered channel notes locally on the device using Apple-provided local storage. This information is not sent to the developer.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications to remind users before selected matches. These notifications are scheduled on the user’s device and are managed by iOS.

Network Access

The app may check a public update file to refresh match labels or schedule information. This request does not include user account information or user-entered channel notes.

Analytics and Tracking

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any tournament organizer, broadcaster, football association, or team. Team names, match times, venues, and viewing labels are shown for factual reference.
```
